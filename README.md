# MathSphere 🌌 - The Ultimate Mathematical Suite

MathSphere is a feature-rich, high-performance mathematical ecosystem built with Flutter. It is designed to be the only tool students, engineers, and financial professionals need to navigate the world of numbers.

## 🎯 The Problem & Solution

**The Problem:** Most users have to switch between 3-4 different apps—a scientific calculator for school, a graphing app for functions, an Excel sheet for financial planning, and a notepad for formulas. This fragmentation breaks focus and makes data tracking impossible.

**The Solution:** **MathSphere** unifies these workflows into a single "Galaxy of Math." It combines 14+ specialized calculators with local data persistence, allowing users to calculate, graph, and document their work in one seamless, privacy-focused environment.

## 🚀 Why MathSphere?

*   **Holistic Architecture:** From simple addition to complex calculus and financial SIP planning.
*   **Privacy-First:** All data (Calculation History, User Formulas, and Notes) is stored locally on your device. No cloud tracking, no data leaks.
*   **Human-Centric UI:** Uses human-readable symbols (ᵐ, ⁿ, √, Δ) and a modern Material 3 design language that adapts to both Light and Dark modes.
*   **Engineered for Precision:** Robust handling of mathematical edge cases (like 0% interest rates or undefined derivative points) to ensure reliability.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📐 Engineering & Pure Math
*   **Calculus Solver:** Symbolic derivatives and numerical integration using Simpson's Rule.
*   **Scientific Suite:** Comprehensive support for trigonometry, logs, powers, and constants (π, e).
*   **Geometry & Trigonometry:** Specialized solvers for shapes, identities, and inverse functions.
*   **Matrix & Complex Numbers:** Advanced linear algebra and imaginary number operations.

### 📊 Data & Probability
*   **Statistics Analysis:** Instant Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation, and Variance for any dataset.
*   **Probability Suite:** Permutations (nPr), Combinations (nCr), and event probability at your fingertips.

### 💰 Finance & Business
*   **Investment Suite:** SIP and Compound Interest calculators with customizable compounding frequencies.
*   **Loan Management:** EMI calculators with detailed breakdowns of total interest and payoff amounts.

### 📓 Productivity & Learning
*   **Dynamic Formula Library:** A massive built-in library of identities plus the ability to create and edit your own.
*   **Smart History:** Every calculation is categorized and logged locally for future reference.
*   **Integrated Notes:** A dedicated space to write down derivations or study plans without leaving the app.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Framework:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev) - For a smooth 60FPS cross-platform experience.
*   **Persistence:** [Hive](https://pub.dev/packages/hive) - Ultra-fast NoSQL local storage for history and notes.
*   **Math Engine:** `math_expressions` & `equations` for high-precision parsing.
*   **Visualization:** `fl_chart` for dynamic function graphing.
*   **Utilities:** `shared_preferences` for theme persistence and `file_saver` for exporting data.
